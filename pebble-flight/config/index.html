How to get your free AeroAPI keyMinimize

Jetebble uses FlightAware’s AeroAPI to track your flights. Here’s how to get started:

  1. Create a FlightAware account.

    Head to flightaware.com to create an account, or sign in if you already have one.

  2. Sign up for AeroAPI.

    Open FlightAware AeroAPI and choose Get started.

  3. Choose Personal.

    Select the Personal tier, which includes up to $5 of free API usage each month.

  4. Complete registration.

    Enter your billing details and finish signing up.

  5. Find your API key.

    Visit the AeroAPI Developer Portal. Your key appears under the Overview tab.

  6. Paste it into Jetebble.

    Copy your key into the field below, add your flights and tap Save settings. You’re ready to go!

A quick note about billing. FlightAware requires a credit card, even for Personal. There’s no fixed monthly subscription fee. Casual personal tracking may fit within the $5 monthly free allowance, but usage beyond it can result in charges. Your cost depends on how much you use the service. Monitor your usage anytime in the AeroAPI Developer Portal, and check current pricing for the latest details.

Keep your key private! Your API key is personal to you and should not be shared.

Flight not showing up yet?

Double-check the flight number and its departure-airport date. Some flights only become available closer to departure. On your watch, press Select on a flight to see its last update and any service message.

Need to try again? Hold Select on a flight and choose Refresh flight. Flight data and gate, terminal or baggage details depend on what FlightAware reports.
